## 3. The "High-Truth, Low-Slop" Client Guarantee

1. **Reality-First Verification:** We never declare a system complete until empirical tests, builds, and live traffic verify performance.
2. **Zero Cargo-Cult Complexity:** We do not recommend trendy frameworks or unnecessary microservices. We choose the simplest architecture that solves the problem robustly.
3. **Transparent Knowledge Transfer:** Clients receive complete architectural documentation, code ownership, and team enablement. No vendor lock-in.
4. **Bilingual & Dual-Timezone Fluency:** Seamless execution across US Pacific / Eastern timezones and Türkiye (UTC+3) business hours.
