# Bilingual Positioning, Copywriting & Voice Standards (EN / TR)

Use this skill when authoring, refining, evaluating, or translating any text, headline, bio, project story, or service offer on Arif Coskun's personal website.

---

## 1. The Core Voice Philosophy: "Engaging, High-Truth, Low-Slop"

The website must be a **joy to read** in both English and Turkish. It is neither a boring corporate resume nor an over-hyped marketing brochure. It is the authentic, charismatic, and deeply knowledgeable voice of a master systems architect and sovereign builder.

### The "Goldilocks" Length Standard
* **Not too verbose:** No wandering paragraphs, zero AI filler words, no historical throat-clearing.
* **Not too short / dry:** Do not reduce rich 20-year experiences to sterile bullet points. Provide the *human context*, the *why*, and the *architectural epiphany*.
* **The Pacing Rule:** Mix short, punchy declarative statements with rhythmic, explanatory sentences.

## 2. English Voice Standard (United States & Global Tech)

### Personality & Cadence
* **Tone:** Direct, confident, peer-to-peer with CTOs and technical founders, conversational yet rigorous.
* **Style:** Silicon Valley / Seattle builder ethos. Real problems, elegant system designs, verifiable code.
* **Banned Words (AI Slop):** `delve`, `leverage`, `utilize`, `synergy`, `furthermore`, `moreover`, `holistic`, `testament`, `game-changer`, `tapestry`, `revolutionize`.

### English Phrasing Examples

#### ❌ Bad (AI Marketing Slop):
> *"In today's fast-paced digital landscape, I leverage cutting-edge AI paradigms to foster transformative synergies and unlock holistic growth across enterprise ecosystems."*

#### ❌ Bad (Too Dry / Stiff):
> *"20 years of IT experience. Azure. SQL. Fabric. MindBall. Contact for consulting."*

#### ✅ Elite (Arif's Authentic English Voice):
> *"Most AI consultants have never managed a production database under heavy load or handled an enterprise cloud incident at 3 AM. For the past 13+ years at beqom, I've architected high-scale compensation data engines and modern Microsoft Fabric lakehouses. By night, I build autonomous multi-agent software factories under a simple rule: software is never done because an AI thinks it's done—it's done when reality agrees."*

## 5. Seven-Sweep Quality Gate for Every Paragraph

Before approving any content in English or Turkish, verify against these 7 sweeps:

1. **Clarity Sweep:** Is the core message understood in under 5 seconds?
2. **Engagement & Flow Sweep:** Does the prose have natural rhythm? Is it pleasant and intriguing to read?
3. **So What? Sweep:** Does every technical feature bridge to a clear strategic or business advantage?
4. **Proof Sweep:** Are assertions backed by concrete evidence (code, architecture, live apps)?
5. **Specificity Sweep:** Are vague generalities replaced by precise numbers (*13+ years, 105 sessions, sub-second latency*)?
6. **Cultural Alignment Sweep:** Does the English resonate with US tech leaders, and does the Turkish resonate naturally with Turkish founders/executives without translation artifacts?
7. **Action Sweep:** Is there a clear, frictionless call to action?
